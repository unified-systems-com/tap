Chaotic Majority
----------------
More things can go wrong than need to go right.
Rightness is known & definable.
Wrongness is elusive and infinitely surprising.

Strictly define what is wrong and incompleteness is a guarantee.
Continuously define what is right and incompleteness is an opportunity.

Known rightness defines the border between success & chaos.
