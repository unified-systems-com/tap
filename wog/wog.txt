Way of the Grid
===============

Lineage
-------
Vanavar Bush once said
It’s the connections between things that are important

Good advice -
nothing exists in a vacuum
- but incomplete.

Because the connections between things...are things.
And the paths over the connections between things...are things too.
So the thing where all the things connect...must be a thing

Somewhere along the way we began calling it the grid

Mindfulness
-----------
A mind is a terrible thing to waste
...on memorizing minutae
...on tracking changes
...on storing complexity

We're built to think

We're meant to act

Be mindful of the direction of information and its rate of flow

The grid remembers
...follow every change,
...contain the complexity,
...stand watch

Your goal is to think as rarely as possible
The grid's purpose is to know and know when it knows not

Your job is to know that the grid knows
...and take appropriate action

----------------

Chaotic Majority
----------------
More things can go wrong than need to go right.
Rightness is known & definable.
Wrongness is elusive and infinitely surprising.

Strictly define what is wrong and incompleteness is a guarantee.
Continuously define what is right and incompleteness is an opportunity.

Known rightness defines the border between success & chaos.
